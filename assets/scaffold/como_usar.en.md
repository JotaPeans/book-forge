## The ideal pace

Consistency beats marathons. Split your study into short sessions on different days.

::: tip Always in this order
1. **Read** the lesson and answer the "Pause and think" boxes before moving on.
2. **Type** every example and run it.
3. **Break** the code on purpose and read the error.
4. **Solve** the exercises without looking at the answer key (it is at the end of the book).
:::
